📱 Lock it down on a phone

✓ Running as an installed app — no address bar or browser buttons.

iPhone / iPad

  1. In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Open Picture Time from the new icon — it runs without the address bar.
  2. Turn on Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access and set a passcode.
  3. Start a slideshow, then triple-click the side (or Home) button → Start. She can't leave the app or swipe home until you triple-click and enter the passcode.

Android

  1. In Chrome, tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen (or Install app). Open Picture Time from the new icon — it runs full-screen.
  2. Turn on Settings → Security → App pinning (search "pin" in Settings) and enable Ask for PIN before unpinning.
  3. Open the Recent apps view, tap the Picture Time icon at the top of its card → Pin. She can't go home or switch apps until you unpin with your PIN.

The app blocks taps that leave the page, long-press menus, pinch zoom, pull-to-refresh and the Back gesture by itself. The phone's own Home swipe can only be locked by Guided Access or App pinning.

While the slideshow runs, links and menus are switched off and Back does nothing. To get out, press and hold the small 🔒 in the top-right corner, then answer the grown-up question. On a computer, ./run.sh opens it in Chrome kiosk mode.
